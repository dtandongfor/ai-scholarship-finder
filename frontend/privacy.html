Privacy at a glance

We use the profile information you enter to generate scholarship recommendations. Resume uploads are processed only to suggest profile fields and are not saved with your profile.

We do not submit applications on your behalf. Scholarship links lead to the official provider, whose privacy terms apply once you leave Intellible.

You can request deletion of your saved profile from the app. Before public launch, publish a support contact and a final legal review of this policy.
